About

I am a physicist working at the interface of artificial intelligence and complex systems. At TECLION, the AI competence center of the Hessian tax administration at Finanzamt Kassel, I develop data-driven AI solutions for the public sector. Part-time, I continue my research as a postdoc at UMG Göttingen. My scientific work combines biophysics and machine learning to understand living systems: stochastic sarcomere dynamics in beating heart cells (eLife), AI-based analysis of sarcomere organization and contractility (SarcAsM), and epithelial tissue morphogenesis through deep-learning microscopy tools (DeepProjection) and physical models of tissue mechanics (PNAS).
